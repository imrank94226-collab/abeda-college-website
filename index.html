About Abeda Inamdar Senior College

The M.C.E. Society is one of the oldest educational organizations of Pune. Established in 1948, the society has played an important role in providing education in various fields.

Abeda Inamdar Senior College of Arts, Science and Commerce, affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University, was established in 1991-92 with B.Sc. and B.Com. courses. The Arts faculty was introduced in 1992-93, followed by several undergraduate, postgraduate and research programmes.

Over the years, the college has grown significantly in terms of courses, students and academic achievements. Its students have received university merit positions and awards, while the college has also been recognized for its academic and social contributions.

The college has worked towards making quality education accessible to students from different sections of society and continues to remain one of the prominent educational institutions of Pune.
