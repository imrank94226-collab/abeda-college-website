Faculty of Arts

The Faculty of Arts at Abeda Inamdar Senior College offers students an opportunity to study humanities and social sciences through a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate programmes.

The Arts faculty includes subjects such as English, Economics, History, Hindi, Marathi, Political Science, Psychology, Sociology, Urdu and Arabic.

The faculty aims to develop students' understanding of society, culture, languages, history and human behaviour while encouraging critical thinking, communication and analytical skills.
